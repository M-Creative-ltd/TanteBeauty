Welcome to Tante Beauty

Tante Beauty is a Rwanda-based nature-inspired enterprise working with smallholder farmers to source medicinal and aromatic plants such as rosemary, moringa, aloe-vera and turmeric.

We transform these natural ingredients into products that support well-being while strengthening sustainable agricultural value chains and improving farmer livelihoods.

Our work connects rural farming communities to value-added production, ensuring that every ingredient is traceable, sustainably grown and part of a fairer and more transparent agricultural system.

Who is Tante and Why Tante?

Tante is Providence Irakoze, the founder of Tante Beauty,  a brand born from curiosity, science and love for natural beauty.

I am a graduate of Food Science and Agribusiness, but my passion was always in fashion and beauty. One day, a lecturer told me; “You seem not to be connected with this course, you need to find a way to combine the two, food science and beauty, that’s where your future lies.” I smiled and asked him, “How?” He replied, “That’s the question you should answer.”

I didn’t understand it then. But years later, when my hair began breaking and nothing seemed to help, I turned to what I knew best, science. Through research and experimentation, I discovered how natural herbs could restore hair health. My first hostelmade oil worked, first on me, then on my friends and family. That’s how Tante Beauty began, from frustration to innovation.

The name Tante is so personal. It’s a nickname my parents gave me when I was born, inspired by the song Laurrete by Kamaliza, where the lyrics go, “Babaza nyina ngo Tantine ari he bazamubwira iki?” My mother loved that part so much when pregnant, she then called me Tante. Over the years, the name has become part of who I am; nurturing, creative, and caring.

Today, I am Tante for my customers, using my background in food science to create safe, natural skincare and haircare products that cure acne, eczema and hyperpigmentation while supporting local farmers to earn fair prices for ingredients like moringa and aloevera that they ususally struggle to sell.

Tante Beauty is a reflection of who I am and what I believe in: Beauty that heals. Science that empowers. Nature that sustains.

Why do we do what we do?

Beauty is how we feel about ourselves, how we love ourselves, how we live our lives, and how we care for one another.

At Tante Beauty, we do what we do because we believe in a world where skincare heals more than skin, it heals stories, communities and confidence. We have seen how local farmers nurture the land with love, yet their harvests often go unseen. We have met women who long to embrace their natural beauty without compromise. That’s why we create products that give back, to your skin and to the people behind every ingredient.

Every time you choose Tante Beauty, you’re choosing a movement of people who love their skin and value people, planet, and purpose in every drop.

Our promise to our clients

Tante Beauty promises care, integrity and respect for you, your skin and the world around us.

Each and every product is made with the best of nature, responsibly sourced and nurtured by farmers who care for the land. We blend science and tradition to create formulas that are gentle, effective and safe for your skin. Guided by sustainability, we reduce waste and honor the planet, while uplifting women and young farmers whose stories inspire each jar/bottle.

Every product you choose from Tante Beauty carries this promise, because beauty should never come at the cost of people or the planet.

This is our promise. Every day, in every drop.
